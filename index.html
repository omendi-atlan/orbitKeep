Bonus Features

File save/load, timestamped logging to station.log, and LINQ reports of modules by lowest O₂.

📏

Domain Rule

EVA allowed only with a buddy astronaut AND O₂ reserve ≥ 40% — otherwise the operation throws.

⚡

Custom Feature

Auto power-shedding: on solar flare, non-essential modules power down and energy reroutes to life support.
